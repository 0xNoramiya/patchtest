# Impact statistics, verified

Every number used in the write-up, the landing page and the video, checked against
the source on 2026-10-31. Where the original brief's phrasing was loose, the
corrected wording is given.

## 1. Children's skincare routines on TikTok

**Study:** Hales M, Rigali S, Paller A, Liszewski W, Lagu T. "Pediatric Skin Care
Regimens on TikTok." *Pediatrics*, 2025. Northwestern University.
**Link:** https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2025/06/250609054355.htm

> "The top-viewed videos contained an average of 11 potentially irritating active ingredients."

> "only 26% of daytime skin care regimens included sunscreen"

**Correction applied.** The 11-ingredient average belongs to the top-viewed videos,
not to every routine by a girl aged 7 to 18. Say "the top-viewed videos", not
"routines by girls aged 7 to 18 averaged".

## 2. Unsafe answers from large language models

**Study:** "Large language models provide unsafe answers to patient-posed medical
questions." *npj Digital Medicine*, 2026, vol 9, article 241, published 13 February.
**Link:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13013898/

> "The model with the highest percent of unsafe responses was GPT-4o (13.5% unsafe)"

Range across the four models: 5.0% (Claude) to 13.5% (GPT-4o). Llama was 13.1%.

**Caveat to state.** Responses were collected in late 2024. Current model versions
may do better, and our own eval found a 0% unsafe rate for the model we used on our
40 scenarios. Quote this as motivation for building a check, not as a claim about
today's models.

## 3. The law that would have required this, and did not pass

**Bill:** California AB 2491 (2024).
**Link:** https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/CA_202320240AB2491

It would have barred selling or delivering over-the-counter anti-aging skin or
cosmetic products containing **vitamin A derivatives or alpha hydroxy acids** to
anyone under 13, required at least one age-verification step, and carried civil
penalties up to $10,000 per day.

> "In committee: Held under submission."

Dated 16 May 2024. It died in committee and never reached a floor vote.

**Why this matters here.** The ingredient classes the bill named are exactly the
ones rule R-AGE-01 blocks for a declared age under 13. The obligation does not
exist in law, so a store that wants it has to build it.
